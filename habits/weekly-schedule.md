## 🔒 The Cascade Protection Protocol (your #1 rule)
Your words: *"if I slip, I slip hard."* One missed thing knocks over the next. Here's how we break the chain:

1. **Fixed wake time: ~7:00am, 7 days a week.** THE keystone. Even weekends, don't drift more than ~45 min. Consistent wake time fixes energy, sleep, and the whole slip pattern.
2. **Fixed lights-out: ~23:00** (≈8h sleep). Your girlfriend heads off ~22:00–22:30, so this fits. Defend it like an appointment.
3. **A missed workout changes NOTHING else.** Miss the gym? Annoying, move on — you do **not** get to stay up late or sleep in. Wake and bedtime are sacred *regardless*. This is the rule that saves your next day.
4. **Never miss twice.** Missed Thursday's gym? The weekend session is non-negotiable. One miss is human; two is the old pattern.
5. **Make-up option:** miss a gym session → do a 20-min home/bodyweight session or a long walk that same day. A miss is never a zero.
