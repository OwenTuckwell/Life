Built around your real week. **Gym is in the mornings** — because you told me evenings are for your girlfriend and that if you leave the gym to the evening and miss it, the whole thing unravels. Mornings remove that risk entirely: it's done before the day can get in the way.

- **Evenings stay yours** for your girlfriend. The system never asks you to trade that.

## 🔒 The Cascade Protection Protocol (your #1 rule)
Your failure mode is the domino chain. Here's how we break it:

1. **Fixed wake time, 7 days a week: ~6:15am.** This is THE keystone. Even Sunday, don't drift more than ~45 min. A consistent wake time fixes energy, sleep, and the whole slip pattern.
2. **Fixed lights-out: ~22:45.** Your girlfriend heads off ~22:00–22:30, so this fits. Defend it like an appointment.
3. **A missed workout changes NOTHING else.** Miss the gym? Annoying, move on — you do **not** get to stay up late or sleep in. The bedtime and wake time are sacred *regardless* of whether the workout happened. This is the rule that saves you.
4. **Never miss twice.** Missed Monday's gym? Thursday is non-negotiable. One miss is human; two is the old pattern.
5. **Make-up option:** if a morning gym gets missed, the fallback is a 20-min home/bodyweight session or a long walk that same day — so a miss is never a zero.

## The breakfast fix (for your 9–10am slump)
You're tired mid-morning almost certainly because you're running on empty. Add breakfast:
- **On gym mornings:** something small before (a banana/coffee), then a proper **protein + carb breakfast after** (eggs + toast, or Greek yoghurt + oats + fruit, or a protein shake + banana).
- **On non-gym mornings:** still eat — protein + carbs within an hour of waking.
- This alone will likely wipe out the 9–10am crash and fuel your workouts.
