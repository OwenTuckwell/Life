# Appearance — The Quick Wins

Appearance is the most *leveraged* of your three fronts: unlike fitness (months) most of this pays off in **days to weeks**, and it's almost all consistency + a few one-time decisions. This is where you get momentum you can *see*, which feeds the harder stuff.

## Daily routine (5–10 min, morning)
- [ ] **Shower / wash face** — clean skin, every day.
- [ ] **Skin basics** — cleanser + moisturizer. Add SPF in the day; it's the #1 thing for how your skin ages. That's the whole routine to start; don't overcomplicate.
- [ ] **Teeth** — brush 2×/day, floss. Genuinely changes your smile and breath = confidence.
- [ ] **Hair + facial hair** — styled or tidy, not just "woke up like this." Beard/stubble deliberately shaped, not neglected.
- [ ] **Clothes that fit** — put on something clean that actually fits you. Fit beats brand every time.
- [ ] **Posture reset** — shoulders back, stand tall. Free, instant, and changes how you're perceived and how you feel.

## This quarter's level-ups (YOUR PICKS)
You chose three, all low-effort and high-impact:

- ✅ **Haircut on a schedule** — action this week: find a good barber (ask around / check reviews), book a cut, and set a recurring booking every 3–4 weeks. A sharp, regular cut is the single biggest appearance upgrade there is.
- ✅ **Skincare basics** — buy three things: a gentle cleanser, a moisturizer, and an SPF moisturizer for daytime. That's the whole kit. Cleanse morning + night, moisturize after, SPF in the day. Results show in a couple of weeks.
- ✅ **Posture project** — this is already built into your gym plan: **face pulls** (Workout B) plus daily thoracic + chest-opener stretches and the "shoulders back, stand tall" reset. Standing tall changes how you look *and* feel, instantly and for free.

## Jawline / under-chin (a conscious spot — here's the plan)
Owen's self-conscious about his jaw/under-chin, especially in side profile. Honest take: structure is fine; the softness is mostly fat + posture + angle, all improvable. No obsessing — this improves as a side effect of the main plan.
- **Lean out** — submental (under-chin) fat is one of the first things to go as body fat drops. The recomp handles this. Biggest lever.
- **Posture** — stand tall, don't jut the head forward, chin slightly forward-and-down. Instantly reveals the jaw. Already a daily cue. Keep tongue resting gently on the roof of the mouth as part of good head posture (helps posture; ignore "mewing" claims about changing bone — that's not real).
- **De-bloat** — good sleep + 2.5–3L water + not too much salt reduces facial puffiness within a week or two.
- **Photo angles** — camera at/above eye level, chin slightly out and down. Car selfies shot from below with the chin tucked are the worst-case angle and not a real baseline.
- **Track it** — include a chin-up side profile in the monthly progress photos (same angle each time) so we see the real change.
