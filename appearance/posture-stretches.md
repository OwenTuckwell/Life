# Posture stretches — the daily 4 (≈4 min)

This is the routine that was referred to vaguely everywhere ("daily thoracic + chest-opener stretches") but never actually written down. Here it is, concrete. Your assessment flagged **mild forward-head / rounded shoulders** from desk time — this undoes it. Do it once a day; the morning (after your walk, before the shower) is the easy slot, but any time counts.

Pair it with the gym work already doing the heavy lifting: **face pulls** (Workout B) + **rows** train the muscles that hold you upright. Stretches open the tight front; the lifts strengthen the weak back. Both, and you stand taller in weeks.

## The four moves
1. **Chin tucks** — ×10. Stand/sit tall, gently draw your head straight back (make a "double chin"), hold 3–5s, release. Retrains the deep neck muscles that fix forward-head. The single most important one for your jaw/side-profile too.
2. **Doorway chest stretch** — 30s. Forearms on a doorframe, elbows ~shoulder height, step one foot through and lean until you feel the stretch across the chest/front-shoulders. Opens the tight pecs that round you forward.
3. **Thoracic extension** — 30s. Sit in a chair, hands behind head, gently arch your upper back over the chair's top edge and breathe. (Or hands-and-knees "cat-cow" ×8 slow reps.) Reverses the desk hunch.
4. **Wall angels** — ×10. Back against a wall, low back / head / arms touching, slide arms up and down like a snow-angel keeping contact. Rebuilds the overhead range rounded shoulders lose.

## Cues to carry all day (free, instant)
- **"Shoulders back, stand tall"** reset — the one already in your daily list.
- **Don't jut the head forward** at the desk/phone; chin slightly back-and-down.
- **Tongue resting gently on the roof of the mouth** — helps head posture (ignore "mewing" bone claims; that part's not real).
- Screen at **eye level** so you're not craning down.

## Tracking
Counts under **"Tidy"/appearance** in the habit tracker — or just tell Claude "did the stretches" at the nightly check-in. Like everything else: the two-minute version (just the chin tucks) still counts on a rushed day. Never miss twice.
