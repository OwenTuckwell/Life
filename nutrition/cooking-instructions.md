# Cooking Instructions (weights, times, method)

Simple, repeatable, no guesswork. Follow any pack instructions first; these are the defaults.

## 🥣 Breakfast — Protein Porridge (daily, ~2 min) — ~400 kcal, 36g protein
**Weights:** 45g oats · ~225ml skimmed milk · 1 scoop whey · handful frozen berries. (No PB — that's now the pre-gym rice cake.)

1. 45g oats + ~225ml milk in a large bowl (it rises).
2. Microwave **~2–2½ min**: **2 min → stir → 20–40s more** until creamy. Watch the last bit so it doesn't boil over.
3. Let it cool ~1 min, **then** stir in the whey (never before/during — heat makes it clump).
4. Stir through berries. Splash of cold milk if too thick.

*Overnight oats version:* 45g oats + ~130ml milk + whey + berries, stir, fridge overnight. Grab and go.

## 🏋️ Pre-gym snack (gym mornings) — ~125 kcal
- 1–2 rice cakes + 15g peanut butter, ~30–45 min before training.

## 🍗 Lunch — Chicken, Rice & Veg (batch of 5, Sunday prep)
**Your numbers:** 1kg frozen chicken cooked & split over 5 boxes (~**200g cooked chicken each**) · **75g dry rice per portion** · handful frozen veg each.

### Chicken (1kg)
- **Oven (easiest for the full bag):** spread on a tray, **200°C fan, ~25–30 min**, turning once. Cook until **piping hot right through, no pink, juices run clear.**
- **Pan:** medium-high, splash of oil, **~10–12 min** in batches, stirring, until cooked through.
- Follow the bag's own instructions if they differ.

### Rice (375g dry = 5 × 75g)
- Rinse, then **375g rice + ~750ml water** (1:2). Bring to boil, cover, **simmer ~10–12 min** until water's absorbed. Fluff with a fork.
- Or 5× single portions: 75g rice + ~150ml water each.

### Veg
- Frozen mixed veg — steam/microwave **~4–5 min**, or boil ~5 min. A big handful per box.

### Build & store
- Split evenly into 5 tubs: 200g chicken + ~200g cooked rice + veg. Season / hot sauce / soy.

## ⚠️ Food safety — important (read once)
Cooked chicken **and** rice keep safely in the fridge for about **3–4 days, not 5.** Rice especially can grow bacteria if left too long. So with a Sunday cook:
- **Keep 3 boxes in the fridge** (eat by Wednesday).
- **Freeze the other 2** as soon as they're cool → move to the fridge the night before you eat them.
- **Cool everything within ~1 hour** of cooking before it goes in the fridge (don't leave it out for hours).
- **Reheat until piping hot / steaming**, and only reheat each box **once**.
- Alternatively: just cook a smaller batch twice a week (e.g. Sun for Mon–Wed, Wed eve for Thu–Fri).

*(This isn't fussiness — reheated rice that's been sat too long is the classic way to give yourself a rough night. Freeze the back half and you're sorted.)*
