90 days is long enough to see real change in your body, a skill, and your habits — and short enough that it doesn't feel infinite. We pick a **small number** of targets. Three or four, not ten.

## The 20% that gets 80% of the result
Most progress comes from a few things done consistently:
- Sleep 7–8h on a regular schedule
- Move / train most days
- Eat mostly whole food, enough protein
- Do the one skill for a short block most days
- Weekly review, every week

Everything else is optimization. Nail these first.
