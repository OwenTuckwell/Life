# 90-Day Plan

Quarter: 2026-07-20 → 2026-10-18

**Theme of this quarter: Become consistent.** Not fittest, not most stylish — *consistent*. If you win the consistency battle, the body and the look follow automatically. Everything below is designed so that showing up beats performing.

Three fronts, chosen from what you told me: **discipline, appearance, fitness.** ~30–60 min/day.

## The targets

1. **Discipline (the keystone): Never miss twice — for 90 days.**
   The single metric that matters. Life happens, you'll miss a day. The rule is you never miss two in a row. Hit this and you've beaten the only thing that's ever stopped you.
   - Measured by: the tracker in `habits/tracker.md`. Target: no two blanks in a row, ever.

2. **Fitness: 3 strength sessions + 8k steps/day, most weeks.**
   - Day 90 = 36+ sessions banked, walking 8k a day on autopilot, visibly stronger (more reps/harder variations than day 1).

3. **Appearance: a locked-in daily grooming + presentation routine, and one "level-up."**
   - Daily routine automatic (see `appearance/routine.md`).
   - One bigger upgrade you choose: e.g. a proper haircut on a schedule, a small set of clothes that actually fit, a skincare basics kit, better posture. Pick 1–2, not all.

## What "done" looks like at day 90
- You have a 90-day tracker that is *mostly green with no double-blanks.*
- You look in the mirror and see someone who's clearly training and clearly takes care of themselves.
- Doing the routine no longer takes willpower — it's just what you do. **That** is the win. The abs are a side effect.

## The 20% that gets 80% of the result
Nail these before optimizing anything:
- Consistent sleep/wake time (this is what fixes energy AND consistency)
- 3 strength sessions + daily walk
- Enough protein, mostly whole food
- Daily grooming routine
- The daily log + weekly review — because for a consistency problem, *tracking is the intervention.*
