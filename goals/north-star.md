# North Star — Who I'm Becoming

> The point of goals isn't to hit a number. It's to become the kind of person for whom that number is normal.

## The person I'm building toward
Someone who does what he says he'll do — whether he feels like it or not. Fit, sharp, put-together, and **disciplined enough to build his own income and a life he owns**, while he's young enough to compound it.

The body and the appearance aren't the destination. They're the **training ground for discipline** — the one skill that transfers to everything else. Master doing the hard, boring thing consistently *here*, then point that same discipline at building something of my own.

### The arc (two phases)
- **Phase 1 — Build the engine (now → ~90 days):** Use fitness, body, and appearance to forge real discipline and consistency. Prove to myself I can set a standard and hold it.
- **Phase 2 — Point it at income (once the discipline is real):** Take the discipline built in Phase 1 and build something of my own — an income, a business, an asset. Get the most out of life while I'm young.

> Order matters: most people try to build the business first and quit because the discipline underneath was never there. I'm building the engine before the car.
