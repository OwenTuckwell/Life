# Day 1 Video — Launch ("I gave AI control of my life for 365 days")

Post: TikTok + YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels (same clip). Keep it **under 45 seconds**. Face-on. Authenticity over polish — a bit nervous is GOOD, it's the story.

## The hook (first 3 seconds — most important part)
Straight to camera, no intro, no "hey guys":
> "I'm about as average as it gets — so for the next 365 days, I'm letting an AI control my life."

## Full script (~40s)
Talk it, don't read it. Bullets in your head, then say it natural. Rough words:

**[Hook — 0:00–0:03, to camera]**
"I'm about as average as it gets. So for the next 365 days, I'm letting an AI control my life."

**[Why — 0:03–0:16]**
"Average fitness, average discipline, average everything — and I'm done with it. So I've handed the whole plan to an AI. It coaches me, it's brutally honest with me, and I do what it says. The goal: get in the best shape of my life, actually build some discipline, and use that to build an income — all on camera, every day, so I can't quietly quit."

**[What got set up — 0:16–0:30, over B-roll]**
"Today we set it all up. It built my training plan, a meal plan on a budget, put my whole week in my calendar — it even called out my posture and my diet. Meal's prepped, gym days locked. And trust me, it does not let me off the hook."

**[Day 1 + CTA — 0:30–0:40]**
"This is Day 1 of 365. Follow along and let's find out: can an AI actually fix an average life… or do I fall apart by week two?"

## Shot list (film these, cut the talking over the B-roll)
- **Talking head** — the whole script, good light on your face, phone at eye level, steady.
- **B-roll to layer in** (record 5–10s of each):
  - Meal prep — chopping / boxing up the chicken, rice & veg
  - Your phone showing the packed calendar (the colourful week)
  - The training/meal plan on screen
  - Whey scoop into the oats
  - Lacing gym trainers / packing the gym bag
  - *(Optional, powerful):* a quick "Day 0" mirror/side profile — your honest before. This becomes gold in a few months.

## Firm notes
- **Film the hook 3 times, keep the most natural one.** Don't do 20 takes of the whole thing — good + posted beats perfect + never.
- **Don't script-read like a robot.** If you fluff a word, leave it in — real is the point.
- **Best post times (UK):** lunch ~12–1pm or evening ~6–9pm.
- **Don't show:** home address, workplace, or your girlfriend/family without asking.
- **Then stop refreshing it.** You posted. That's the win. The numbers don't matter on Day 1.
