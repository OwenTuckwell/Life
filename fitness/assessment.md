## Baseline — 2026-07-21

**Starting point:** Untrained, "skinny-fat" lean toward soft. Not overweight. Good natural frame with above-average shoulder width and a solid shoulder-to-waist ratio (the genetic bit you can't train — and it's there).

**Body fat estimate:** ~18–22% (rough, from photos in warm lighting).

**Strengths to build on:**

- Not much fat to lose → this is a recomposition, not a big cut.
- Young + untrained → prime position for "newbie gains" (build muscle + lose fat at once for the first 6–12 months).

**Priorities (in order):**
1. Build muscle everywhere — shoulders, back, chest, arms (the gym plan handles this).
2. Lean out lower belly / love handles — slight calorie deficit + daily steps. No crash diet.
3. Posture — mild forward-head/rounded look; face pulls + daily thoracic/chest openers + "stand tall" resets.

**Realistic 90 days:** leaner midsection, fuller shoulders/arms/chest, better posture, meaningfully stronger. Not chasing visible abs (that's a 6–12 month job) — chasing consistency and letting the body follow.
